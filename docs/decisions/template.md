# NNNN. Title of the decision

Status: Proposed, Accepted, or Superseded by NNNN.
Date: YYYY-MM-DD.

## Context

What forces the decision: the need, the constraints, and the facts that the
choice depends on. State the facts that, if they change, would make the
decision wrong.

## Decision

The choice, in one or two sentences, in the active voice: "We use ...".

## Alternatives rejected

Each option that was considered, and the reason it was not chosen.

## Consequences

What is easier and what is harder after the decision. Include the follow-up
work and the conditions under which to review the decision.
